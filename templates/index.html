🧩 The Core Scheduling Challenge

80% of brands rely on a single dedicated employee handling all processes (Customer Support & Abandoned Carts). When that employee takes their mandatory weekly off or planned leave, the brand faces immediate operational downtime.

SmartRoster solves this with smart pod-level cross-coverage, multi-strategy generation (Balanced vs. Client-Priority), and a live conflict validation engine.

🎯 Three Scheduling Strategies

  • Balanced (Strict Weekly Off): Strictly preserves employee weekly offs; flags coverage gaps for single-resource brands and recommends pod backups.
  • Auto-Backup (Pod-Assisted): Automatically searches and schedules available same-pod peers to cover gaps.
  • Client-Priority (365-Day Operations): Prioritizes mandatory client SLA over preferred weekly offs; flags comp-off warnings for manager review.
